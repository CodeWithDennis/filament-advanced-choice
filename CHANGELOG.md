## [1.0.0] - 2026-04-17

### Added

- Open source release under the MIT License.
- Form components: `RadioList`, `RadioCards`, `RadioStackedCards`, `RadioTable`, `CheckboxList`, `CheckboxCards`, `CheckboxStackedCards`, `CheckboxTable`.
- `extras()` for per-option secondary content; enum support via `HasExtra`.
- `hiddenInputs()`, `visibleInputs()`, and `hiddenInputIcon()` for card-style layouts.
- Searchable options on all variants; checkbox variants support `bulkToggleable()`.
